# Fidibeki — Distribution Beyond Cold Email

**Date:** 2026-07-27  
**Status:** Proposed operating plan — no third-party posts or accounts created  
**Owner:** Inês, co-founder

## Outcome

Build a repeatable, no-paid-ads path to the first ten paid subscriptions without making Fidibeki dependent on a LinkedIn account or on cold email alone. Cold email remains a careful, continuous conversation channel; it is not the content strategy.

## The operating mix

| Motion | Purpose | Weekly cadence | Success signal |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Human outbound | Start a few relevant conversations in the US and UK | 5–10 new, individually qualified first touches after deliverability review | Relevant replies, trials, paid conversions |
| Product-studio partners | Reach several SaaS teams through teams that launch and maintain them | 3 researched partner conversations | A referral, introduction, or repeatable handover opportunity |
| Founder proof in public | Make the problem legible before asking for a meeting | 1 useful founder note + 5 thoughtful replies in public communities | Qualified profile/site visits and conversations |
| Searchable practical asset | Give product teams something useful even if they never reply | 1 small asset every two weeks | Organic visits, saves, sign-ups, and replies that cite it |
| Customer learning loop | Turn every live trial into better proof and onboarding | One activation interview per live customer | A verified lesson, quote, or introduction with permission |

## What “public” means without LinkedIn

No LinkedIn access is required. The first test should use places where a founder can participate with a real account and a useful contribution, such as Indie Hackers, Hacker News “Show HN” only when there is a genuine product update, founder/product communities, relevant Slack or Discord groups where promotion is permitted, and product-launch directories. We do not create accounts, submit listings, or post promotional content without the founder's explicit approval and account access.

The tone is not “try Fidibeki.” It is practical: share what makes a bug report actionable, how small teams decide whether feedback is a bug or a request, and how to close the loop with the person who reported it.

## First three assets to validate

These are hypotheses, not promised content. They are intentionally tied to a problem Fidibeki already solves and can be made without invented customer claims.

1. **The five details that make a user-reported bug actionable** — a short checklist for product/support teams; useful in a founder post and as a simple page on fidibeki.com.
2. **A lightweight feedback triage sheet for a two-person product team** — a downloadable/plain-text framework showing report, evidence, decision, owner, and reporter update.
3. **What to say when a user reports a problem you cannot reproduce** — a short founder note and help-centre-style template that demonstrates empathy and evidence gathering.

Before publishing, each asset needs a product/technical accuracy check and a single owner for replies. The asset should lead naturally to Fidibeki, but stand alone if the reader never signs up.

## Geographic application

- **United States:** continue narrow corporate outreach with the required commercial footer and BCC; do not resend any contact already marked “do not resend.”
- **United Kingdom:** build the partner lane first; send only after the jurisdiction, corporate-subscriber, transparency, suppression, BCC, and one-time-send checks are recorded.
- **Rest of Europe:** use community participation, customer introductions, inbound/search assets, and consented/warm contacts until the country-specific direct-marketing rule is documented. Germany is excluded from cold email without prior express consent.
- **Africa/Angola:** keep only opportunistic, high-fit conversations while USD/EUR payment friction remains unresolved.

## Weekly review

Every Friday, compare channels by paid or activated outcomes—not opens, impressions, or follower counts:

1. Which message or public observation generated a real product conversation?
2. Where did a prospect become stuck: relevance, trial setup, widget installation, or payment?
3. Which channel produced a qualified trial or introduction at the lowest founder effort?
4. What should stop next week because it did not produce learning or demand?

## Decisions needed before execution beyond email

- Select the first public channel/account that the founder is comfortable using, or confirm that Inês should prepare posts for the founder to publish manually.
- Approve one of the three practical assets for creation on fidibeki.com.
- Nominate who answers any public replies and product questions within one business day.
