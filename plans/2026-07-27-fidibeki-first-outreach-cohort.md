## Purpose

This is a deliberately small, evidence-backed starting cohort. It is not a bulk list. Every contact below is a business address published by the organisation itself; no personal addresses, purchased data, or inferred email patterns are included.

The cohort contains two motions:

1. **Customer candidates** that plausibly fit Fidibeki Growth or Scale because they operate a web software product with multiple user journeys.
2. **Referral partners** that build or support SaaS products and can introduce Fidibeki where a client's product needs an in-product feedback loop.

No message may be sent until it has an individual draft, a recorded personalisation rationale, a suppression check, and `acidiney.dias@fidibeki.com` in BCC.

## Customer candidates

### 1. GereOnline / Softline Angola

- **Public business contact:** `contacto@softlineangola.com`
- **Website:** https://www.gereonline.com/
- **Fit score:** 8/10
- **Why it fits:** GereOnline describes itself as a modular SaaS suite serving education, health, HR, and business operations. Its product includes separate portals for students, teachers, and guardians, creating many distinct user journeys where an in-product feedback loop can reduce support back-and-forth.
- **Best target role:** Product owner, engineering lead, or operations leader responsible for the product experience.
- **Honest conversation starter:** The public product describes several portal audiences. Ask how product feedback from those different users currently reaches the team, especially when the report needs context beyond a written description.
- **Fidibeki relevance:** A Growth evaluation can show role-aware widget placement, structured feedback, attachments, optional replay where appropriate, triage, and reporter updates.
- **Research source:** https://www.gereonline.com/
- **Status:** Draft only; do not send until a product-facing recipient or routing confirmation is found.

### 2. Kacennu

- **Public business contact:** `apoio.cliente@kacennu.ao`
- **Website:** https://kacennu.ao/
- **Fit score:** 7/10
- **Why it fits:** Kacennu is an Angolan browser-based SaaS product with multi-company and multi-store workflows. It explicitly supports a broad operational customer base and a Portuguese-language support channel.
- **Best target role:** Product owner, founder, or customer-experience lead.
- **Honest conversation starter:** Its product promises a quick online setup for a multi-company system. Ask how the team separates actionable product feedback from ordinary support requests when users report friction in a browser workflow.
- **Fidibeki relevance:** The first conversation is about improving the evidence and feedback-to-decision loop, not replacing its customer-support channel.
- **Research source:** https://kacennu.ao/
- **Status:** Research a product-facing business address before any send; the published address is clearly support-oriented.

### 3. AgroKuia

- **Public business contact:** `contact@agrokuia.com`
- **Website:** https://www.agrokuia.com/
- **Fit score:** 6/10
- **Why it fits:** AgroKuia presents an AI agricultural product available on web, Android, and iOS, with a stated pilot and planned expansion. The web surface and active product-development stage make it a possible early Growth candidate.

- **Research source:** https://www.agrokuia.com/

### 4. Climber RMS

- **Public business contact:** `sales@climberrms.com`
- **Website:** https://www.climberrms.com/
- **Fit score:** 6/10
- **Why it fits:** Climber RMS operates a SaaS product for hotel revenue teams, with a published Portugal presence, a visible product team, support terms, and ongoing product releases. It is larger and more mature than the core first-customer profile, so it is an upside test rather than the first priority.

- **Research sources:** https://www.climberrms.com/en/contact/ and https://climberrms.com/sobre/
