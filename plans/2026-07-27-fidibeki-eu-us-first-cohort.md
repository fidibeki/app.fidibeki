## Purpose

Europe and the United States are the primary markets for the first paid-subscription motion. This deliberately tiny starting cohort tests two different routes:

1. A small European SaaS team with a visible support-to-product feedback problem.
2. A Europe/US-facing product studio that can become a referral partner after it has assessed Fidibeki's fit for its clients.

The cohort is research, not a sending queue. Each prospect needs an individual draft, an up-to-date public-address check, a suppression check, direct-marketing review for its jurisdiction, a send-log entry, and `acidiney.dias@fidibeki.com` in BCC before delivery.

## 1. Glassdesk — customer-discovery candidate (Germany)

- **Public business contact:** `hello@glass-desk.com`
- **Website:** https://glass-desk.com/
- **Fit score:** 7/10
- **What is verified:** Glassdesk presents itself as a small Berlin team building an AI customer-support copilot for SaaS teams. Its site says the team answers its own tickets, maintains a public changelog, and uses separate EU and US regions for ticket data.
- **Why Fidibeki may fit:** Support-ticket work is adjacent to, but different from, deciding what to fix or build. A team handling its own tickets may value a more structured way to turn recurring in-product reports into evidence, a decision, and a reporter update.
- **Truthful opening angle:** Ask how recurring product feedback found in support conversations currently reaches the product/engineering decision process when the original report needs product context. Do not imply that Glassdesk has a problem or that its support workflow is inadequate.
- **Caution:** This is a discovery conversation, not a claim that Fidibeki replaces customer support or their product. The public mailbox may be shared; address the note to the team, not a guessed individual.
- **Research source:** https://glass-desk.com/
- **Status:** Worth one individually reviewed draft after suppression and Germany/EU direct-marketing checks.

## 2. Wedece — referral-partner candidate (Europe/US-facing)

- **Public business contact:** `contact@wedece.com`
- **Website:** https://wedece.com/
- **Fit score:** 8/10 as a partner, not as a subscription prospect
- **What is verified:** Wedece describes itself as a small senior team that designs, ships, and runs web and SaaS products for founders and product leaders. It explicitly offers ongoing product support and says it works across EU, US, and APAC time zones.
- **Why Fidibeki may fit:** Its work continues after launch, which is the point at which a client needs a reliable way to carry context-rich end-user feedback back into product decisions. A referral partner could create a better path to several qualifying SaaS teams than a generic volume campaign.
- **Truthful opening angle:** Ask whether Wedece already gives selected post-launch SaaS clients a structured way to bring end-user issue and feature reports—with useful context—back to the build team. Offer a short look at Fidibeki only if the answer is relevant.
- **Caution:** Do not position Fidibeki as a replacement for its product-support or delivery work; ask about an optional post-launch layer for its clients.
- **Research source:** https://wedece.com/
- **Status:** Worth one individually reviewed partnership draft after suppression and contact-location review.

## 3. SolarFleet — later customer-discovery candidate (United Kingdom)

- **Public route:** Contact page; no public business email collected yet.
- **Website:** https://solarfleet.io/company/about
- **Fit score:** 7/10
- **What is verified:** SolarFleet describes a small Cardiff team building active-development software for UK commercial solar operators, publishing product changes in a public changelog and operating its own product in a real O&M business.
- **Why Fidibeki may fit:** A product used in operationally complex workflows is likely to receive feedback that benefits from context and traceability. The company is a useful European customer-discovery candidate once a clearly appropriate public business route is confirmed.
- **Truthful opening angle:** Its team says releases are tried in its own operations before customers' accounts. Ask how field feedback from customer accounts is separated from operational support and carried into product decisions.
- **Caution:** Do not use a guessed email pattern or contact form for a cold sales sequence. Research the published contact route and applicable UK direct-marketing requirements first.
- **Research source:** https://solarfleet.io/company/about
- **Status:** Research only.

## Exclusions

- Feedback, roadmap, and changelog vendors are excluded as direct customer prospects because they overlap Fidibeki's category.
- Larger procurement-led companies are not part of this initial test.
- Africa/Angola is not in this primary cohort because USD/EUR payment friction makes commercial fit less certain. Such prospects remain exceptional, not volume targets.
