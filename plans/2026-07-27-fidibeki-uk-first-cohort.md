## UK operating basis

This cohort uses only public, role-neutral `hello@` addresses published by companies that identify themselves as UK limited companies. No named contact or personal business address is stored or used.

- The companies are corporate subscribers, not sole traders or unincorporated partnerships, based on their own legal/company disclosures.
- The purpose is a narrowly relevant business introduction about Fidibeki's in-product feedback workflow, sent once to a published general work inbox.
- Processing necessity and balance: the relevant public business inbox is the least intrusive route; each note is manually researched, short, untracked, identifies Fidibeki, and gives a simple opt-out.
- No personal-data profile, purchased list, pixel, attachment, or automatic follow-up is used.
- An unsubscribe, objection, or “not interested” response is added to the permanent suppression list immediately.

This is an operational assessment, not legal advice. It follows the ICO's current guidance that electronic-mail consent is not required under PECR for corporate subscribers, while identity, a valid opt-out route, UK GDPR fairness/transparency where personal data is processed, and objections still matter: [ICO B2B marketing guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/).
