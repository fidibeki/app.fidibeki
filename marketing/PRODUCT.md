# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Fidibeki serves product teams collecting feedback from people using their websites and apps. The marketing homepage introduces the product to prospective customers. Audience roles beyond the existing product, engineering and customer-support use cases remain open.

## Product Purpose

Collect contextual feedback, understand reports, triage and track issues, and communicate outcomes to reporters who opted in. Website conversations let tenant teams contact their own visitors and optionally accept visitor-started messages.

## Operating Context

Visitors evaluate the product before starting a trial or booking a demo. Existing marketing routes explain features, integrations, pricing, installation, security and privacy. Signup and sign-in use the existing app links.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Use the checked-in feature and pricing catalogs as the source of product facts. Screenshots, audio, replay and AI depend on configuration, permissions, consent and plan eligibility. Chat is project-scoped. Do not invent customer counts, testimonials, benchmarks or unverified claims. The approved homepage design now extends to every marketing route. Preserve route content, pricing, installation instructions, legal terms and functionality. Run on a local dev server; do not deploy.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the Fidibeki name and existing mark. The user chose OpenAI's marketing website as the reference for clean typography, spacious composition and dynamic presentation. The previous homepage's visual treatment is being replaced for this local concept.

## Evidence on Hand

`data/features.ts`, `data/pricing.ts`, `data/site.ts` and `data/integrations.ts` contain existing facts and destinations. Tauview is the confirmed customer in the existing catalog. Local brand assets live in `public/brand`. Product demonstrations must be identified as examples. No new customer quotation has been supplied.

## Product Principles

- Show the path from feedback to a decision and a useful reply.
- Keep customer words and choices central.
- Distinguish illustration from customer evidence.
- Make the trial and product details easy to find.
